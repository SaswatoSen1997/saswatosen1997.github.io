## Statistical fields on networks

### Critical phenomena on graphs

My doctoral work studies how geometry and graph spectra affect the critical behavior of interacting scalar field theories. Together with my collaborators, I use lattice perturbation theory and the functional renormalization group to study short- and long-range interactions on the Bethe lattice. Our preprint examines how critical behavior depends on the range of the interaction and on the spectral properties of the kinetic operator.

[Critical Phenomena on the Bethe Lattice (arXiv)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01961)

### Dirac walks on regular trees

With Nicolas Delporte and Reiko Toriumi, I used the recursive structure of regular trees to study Dirac walks, which move between the vertices and edges of an oriented graph. We obtained exact results for the Green functions and studied the spectrum and spectral dimension.

[Dirac walks on regular trees (arXiv)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10881)

## AI research

My current interests include learning dynamics, mechanistic interpretability, deep learning theory, and technical AI safety. I am exploring synthetic datasets with a known hierarchical or fractal structure. These models give me a way to ask which levels of structure a neural network learns and how that depends on properties of the data.

## Asymptotic safety in quantum gravity

At Heidelberg University, I worked on higher-derivative quantum gravity using the functional renormalization group. The collaborations studied asymptotic freedom, asymptotic safety, and scaling solutions.

[Asymptotic freedom and safety in quantum gravity (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP03%282022%29130) · [Scaling solutions for asymptotically free quantum gravity (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP02%282023%29054)
